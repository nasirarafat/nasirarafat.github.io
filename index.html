Four areas of work, one governed foundation

AI is only as safe, useful and adoptable as the data underneath it. Each engagement draws on these areas to build that foundation and put it to work.

Data literacy

A data-fluent organisation

Teams that trust, question and use data confidently, rather than just receiving dashboards.

Often a fit for: Teams who rely on reports but cannot yet question them.

  • Data literacy programmes and microsites
  • Self-serve analytics enablement
  • Stakeholder training
  • Data culture design

AI safety

Guardrails that hold up under scrutiny

Risk-aware, auditable AI adoption, built for regulated and high-consequence environments.

Often a fit for: Regulated teams that must show how an AI decision was reached.

  • AI risk and impact assessments
  • Governance frameworks and access controls
  • Model and data drift monitoring
  • Audit-ready documentation

AI agents

Agentic workflows on governed data

Automating real operational decisions, not just chat interfaces.

Often a fit for: Operations teams drowning in alerts and manual review.

  • Agent architecture on trusted data layers
  • Anomaly detection and decision-support agents
  • Human-in-the-loop workflow design
  • Integration with existing BI and operations tooling

AI readiness

The foundation AI needs to scale

Assessing and building the architecture, quality and governance behind every AI use case.

Often a fit for: Organisations with AI ambitions and an unclear data estate.

  • Data and cloud architecture assessment
  • Data quality and lineage foundations
  • Roadmap from raw data to AI-ready platform
  • Vendor and platform selection support

How an engagement runs

The same sequence applies whether it is a two-week readiness assessment or a multi-year technical lead role.

Assess

Audit sources, quality, architecture and governance maturity against the use case.

Govern

Establish ownership, lineage, quality controls and safety boundaries first.

Enable

Train teams and ship self-serve tools that make governed data usable.

Scale

Roll out agents and AI capability on a foundation that is already governed and adopted.

Nasir Arafat is a Microsoft Certified Data Engineer with over ten years of combined industry and academic experience in big data, analytics and AI, focused on renewable energy.

As Data and AI Technical Lead for the Renewables Centre of Excellence, he delivers data products, AI solutions and insight capabilities that turn complex engineering and operational data into trusted, governed intelligence. He works as both project manager and data subject matter expert across cloud platforms, big data engineering, architecture, governance and AI-ready foundations.

He contributes to the wider data community through CDOXUK, Network Group, PyData and DataNext Europe, and mentors with The Data Lab. He is also a qualified first aider, a British Council GREAT Scholar and a recipient of the King's Award for Voluntary Service.
